Wrong warping is a glitch only present in the PC version of the game, which allows the player to be warped to incorrect fields, most notably allowing for access to the [debug room](/index.php/Debug_Room "Debug Room").

## Execution

In order to perform the wrong warp, the player must trigger a game over on a screen with a battle flag that sends the player to the field module at some point. The player must then load a save on the world map, where the battle flag will still be loaded in memory. Then, after entering a fight and completing the conditions for returning to the field (usually winning the fight), the game will erroneously send the player to the field module instead of correctly sending them back to the world map.

There are a few variations on this glitch:

  * [Mt. Nibel Wrong Warp](/index.php/Mt._Nibel_Wrong_Warp "Mt. Nibel Wrong Warp")
  * [Reactor 1 Warp](/index.php/Reactor_1_Warp "Reactor 1 Warp")
  * [Yuffie Warp](/index.php/Yuffie_Warp "Yuffie Warp")
