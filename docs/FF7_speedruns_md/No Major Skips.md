No Major Skips is a speedrun category that prohibits the following large skips:

  * [Guard Skip](/index.php/Guard_Skip "Guard Skip")
  * [Kalm Skip](/index.php?title=Kalm_Skip&action=edit&redlink=1 "Kalm Skip \(page does not exist\)")
  * [Cosmo Canyon Skip](/index.php?title=Cosmo_Canyon_Skip&action=edit&redlink=1 "Cosmo Canyon Skip \(page does not exist\)")
  * [Raid on Midgar Skip](/index.php?title=Raid_on_Midgar_Skip&action=edit&redlink=1 "Raid on Midgar Skip \(page does not exist\)")
  * [Turks Skip](/index.php?title=Turks_Skip&action=edit&redlink=1 "Turks Skip \(page does not exist\)")

The category also prohibits Cait Sith's [Slots](/index.php?title=Slots&action=edit&redlink=1 "Slots \(page does not exist\)") limit break.
