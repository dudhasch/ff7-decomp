The first segment of this page links to all manipulations that can be used by each category of speedrun. The remaining segments detail all known manipulations.

### All Bosses (Warps)

## Midgar

Manipulations from Midgar to the Chocobo Ranch or to Kalm will have to contend with entering the Zolom Box partway through he traversal.

### to Chocobo Ranch

Due to the Zolom Box, it is more difficult but still feasible to manipulate a 0-encounter traversal from Midgar all the way to the Chocobo Ranch.

#### Slots / No Slots

High-level runs on PlayStation in categories that do not perform [Guard Skip](/index.php/Guard_Skip "Guard Skip") will typically reach this point with an IGT between around 1:29 to 1:35.

#### All Bosses / 100%

### to Kalm

A 0-encounter traversal manipulation to Kalm can be performed by moving right from Midgar and up-right to Kalm.

The difficulty with this manipulation is that exactly when switching from right to up-right can affect how many frames it takes to reach the western edge of the Zolom box, meaning RNG could progress differently afterwards. It isn't difficult to find solutions that work for many different positions, but a smaller set of positions for this allows for more IGTs that work. More IGTs can be generated by allowing menu buffers towards the beginning of the traversal.

##### No Major Skips

## Kalm

The spawn location when exiting Kalm is just above but not quite within the Zolom Box. Moving down slightly will enter the box, however.

### to Mythril Mines

Runs that visit Kalm but do not visit the Chocobo Ranch next go to the Mythril Mines and therefore do Zolom Skip in order to pass through the marsh. The traversal to the swamp can be manipulated to have no encounters and to have the Zolom in a favorable location for a faster or safer Zolom Skip.

#### No Major Skips

## Chocobo Ranch

The spawning location from exiting the Chocobo Ranch is within the Zolom Box.

### to Mythril Mines

Runs that collect Chocobo Lure or otherwise visit the ranch but do not perform Kalm Skip would often perform a manipulation to get past the Zolom and go to Mythril Mines. This is no longer useful in any main-category runs, but it was used in runs that performed the original method of Cosmo Canyon Skip. The closest to a main-category run that this manipulation might be useful for may be 100% NMS / Glitchless.

### Chocobo Encounter

Runs that perform Kalm Skip will manipulate a fight with a Chocobo that is caught and then used to perform the skip. In order to be able to consistently catch the Chocobo without needing to purchase or use Greens, a specific formation is manipulated that consists either of a Chocobo and two Madragora enemies or, to fill gaps between those formations, a Chocobo, a Levikron, and a Madragora. The enemies in these fights can be defeated with two or three total grenade throws.

## Corel (Desert)

Corel Desert seems to take longer to load the world map on PSX than other transitions (resulting in a 5-second menu offset on PSX instead of the usual 3-second offset).

### Beachplugs Encounter

Runs that obtain Big Guard may choose to manipulate a Beachplugs encounter from a beach near the Gold Saucer to guarantee that the first encounter isn't against a Grangalan enemy.

The chance of an encounter with beachplugs is 75%, so it's arguable how important this manipulation is. However, it is possible to perform a manipulation that pairs well with the setup for PSX [Cosmo Canyon Skip](/index.php?title=Cosmo_Canyon_Skip&action=edit&redlink=1 "Cosmo Canyon Skip \(page does not exist\)").

### Harpy / Flapbeats Encounter

Runs that obtain Aqualung or need to grind enemy kills may manipulate a Harpy or Flapbeats encounter using the Gold Saucer desert.

#### Chain Manipulation

Slots typically needs to obtain Aqualung and then defeat sets of Flapbeats. As such, it is possible to chain-manipulate a Flapbeats fight by exiting the harpy fight, which would ideally be a preemptive encounter.
