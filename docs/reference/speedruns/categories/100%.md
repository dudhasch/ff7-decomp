100% is a speedrun category defined by requiring all [items](/index.php?title=Item&action=edit&redlink=1 "Item \(page does not exist\)") and [Materia](/index.php?title=Materia&action=edit&redlink=1 "Materia \(page does not exist\)") to be obtained, all bosses to be defeated, all playable characters recruited, all limits learned, and all sidequests to be completed.

## Requirements

### Items

  * Collect all usable Items, Weapons, Armor, Accessories. (Exceptions: Key Items, Limit Manuals, Desert Rose, Earth Harp)
  * All Items must be in the Inventory when finishing the game. (Exception: Save Crystal)

### Materia

  * Collect all Magic, Command, Summon, Support and Independent Materia.
  * All 4 Enemy Skill Materia must be obtained and mastered. No other Materia has to be mastered.
  * All Materia must be in the inventory when finishing the game.

### Sidequests

  * Gold Chocobo: Breed a Golden Chocobo at the Chocobo Ranch.
  * Kalm Traveler: Give the Guide Book, Earth Harp and Desert Rose to the Traveler.
  * Turtle Paradise: Obtain the items from the Turtle Paradise barkeeper.
  * Wutai: Defeat Rapps and reobtain your stolen Materia
  * Pagoda: Defeat Godo in the Pagoda
  * Lucrecia: Talk to Lucrecia behind the Waterfall
  * Battle Square: Win the Special Battle.
  * Villa de Cloud: Purchase the Villa in Costa del Sol for 300.000 Gil

  * Huge Materia: Obtain all 4 Huge Materia from Fort Condor, Corel, Submarine and the Rocket. They don’t need to be placed into Bugenhagens observatory.

### Playable Characters

  * Cloud
  * Barret
  * Tifa
  * Aerith
  * Red XIII
  * Yuffie
  * Cait Sith
  * Vincent
  * Cid

### Limits

  * All Characters (including Aerith) need to have all of their Limit Breaks learned.

### Verification

  * When all requirements have been met the following things have to be shown:
    * Remove All Materia, Arrange and scroll through them
    * Sort Items by “Type” and scroll through them
    * Show every characters Equipment and Limits (with the exception of Aerith)

  * Save Crystal can be set in the last room before the Final Descend and a save made.
  * After the run the save can be loaded and the run be verified as shown above.
