# Knights of Round Materia

> *Summons Knights of Round*

## Overview

The strongest attack in the game: Ultimate End is thirteen consecutive non-elemental hits that ignore Magic Defense. Also the most expensive Materia to master at 500,000 AP, and the only Materia that Quadra Magic refuses to link with. Often called Knights of the Round.
