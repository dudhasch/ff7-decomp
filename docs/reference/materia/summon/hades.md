# Hades Materia

> *Summons Hades*

## Overview

Black Cauldron deals non-elemental damage to all enemies and inflicts eight statuses at 100% on anything not immune. Note that although the summon also inflicts Slow and Paralyzed, those two are not traits of the Materia itself, so Added Effect cannot transfer them.
