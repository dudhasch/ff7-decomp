# Materia

Reference notes on every Materia in Final Fantasy VII (PS1, USA), compiled from the Final Fantasy Wiki. One file per Materia: in-game description, stat changes, level/AP table, what it grants, and where to find it.

There are five categories, distinguished in game by colour.

| Category | Colour | Count | What it does |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| [Magic](magic/README.md) | Green | 22 | Grants the Magic command and its spells |
| [Summon](summon/README.md) | Red | 17 | Grants the Summon command, limited castings per battle |
| [Command](command/README.md) | Yellow | 14 | Adds a battle command such as Steal, Morph or Mime |
| [Support](support/README.md) | Blue | 13 | Modifies a Materia in a linked slot |
| [Independent](independent/README.md) | Purple | 17 | Standalone support abilities and stat boosts |

Dummied (unused) Materia are documented alongside their category: 3 in Magic, 1 in Command, 1 in Support.

## Shared mechanics

- **Stat changes.** Magic and Summon Materia trade physical stats for magical ones. The penalties scale with the Materia's power: a basic Fire is -2% Max HP / -1 Strength, Knights of Round is -10% Max HP / +8 Magic.
- **AP and mastering.** Materia gain AP from battle and gain a star at each threshold. A mastered Materia reproduces itself, yielding a fresh level-1 copy.
- **Master Materia.** Master Magic, Master Summon and Master Command each collapse a whole category into one slot, and are built by bringing mastered copies of every Materia in that category to the matching Huge Materia in Cosmo Canyon, or by trading the Earth Harp to the Kalm traveler.
- **Elements and statuses.** A Materia's Element and Status columns matter mainly for [Elemental](support/elemental.md) and [Added Effect](support/added-effect.md) pairings, not for what the Materia itself casts.
