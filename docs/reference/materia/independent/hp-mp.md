# HP<->MP Materia

## Overview

Swaps max HP and max MP outright. On a dedicated caster this converts a large HP pool into an enormous MP pool; it is also a well-known way to break the MP cap in combination with HP Plus.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | [Independent Materia](README.md) (purple) |
| **Max level** | 2 |
| **AP to master** | 80,000 |

## Stat changes

None.

## Levels

| Level | Effect | AP to reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Exchanges the character's HP and MP values. | 0 |
| Master | No further effect. | 80,000 |

## How to obtain

- Materia Cave north of Corel
