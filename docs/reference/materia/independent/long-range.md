# Long Range Materia

## Overview

Removes the back-row damage penalty and the out-of-reach restriction. Lets a physical attacker sit in the back row taking half damage while dealing full damage.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | [Independent Materia](README.md) (purple) |
| **Max level** | 2 |
| **AP to master** | 80,000 |

## Stat changes

None.

## Levels

| Level | Effect | AP to reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Attacks do full damage regardless of row, and can reach enemies normally out of melee range. | 0 |
| Master | No further effect. | 80,000 |

## How to obtain

- Mythril Mine
