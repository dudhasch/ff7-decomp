# Pre-Emptive Materia

## Overview

Increases the odds of starting a battle with a full ATB gauge and the enemy unable to act. The Ambush/Back Attack protection at Master is an obscure but real defensive perk.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | [Independent Materia](README.md) (purple) |
| **Max level** | 5 |
| **AP to master** | 80,000 |

## Stat changes

| Stat | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Dexterity | +2 |

## Levels

| Level | Effect | AP to reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +6% chance of a pre-emptive strike. | 0 |
| 2 | +9%. | 8,000 |
| 3 | +11%. | 20,000 |
| 4 | +13%. | 40,000 |
| Master | +19%; stacks with additional Pre-Emptive Materia up to +33%. A 5-star Pre-Emptive also halves the chance of Ambush and Back Attack formations, but only when placed on the third (last) character. | 80,000 |

## How to obtain

- Battle Square prize
- Equipped on Cloud during "The Tragedy of Five Years Ago" flashback
