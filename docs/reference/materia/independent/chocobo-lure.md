# Chocobo Lure Materia

## Overview

Required to encounter chocobos at all, and therefore a prerequisite for the entire chocobo breeding chain - which is in turn the only route to Knights of Round. Also required to learn the Chocobuckle enemy skill.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | [Independent Materia](README.md) (purple) |
| **Max level** | 4 |
| **AP to master** | 30,000 |

## Stat changes

| Stat | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Luck | +1 |

## Levels

| Level | Effect | AP to reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Chocobos can be encountered near chocobo tracks on the world map. | 0 |
| 2 | 1.5x chance of encountering chocobos. | 3,000 |
| 3 | 2x chance. | 10,000 |
| Master | 2x chance; stacks with additional Chocobo Lure Materia up to 4x. | 30,000 |

## How to obtain

- Purchase at the Chocobo Farm
- Found outside the Chocobo Farm after acquiring the Highwind
