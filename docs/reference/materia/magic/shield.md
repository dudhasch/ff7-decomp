# Shield Materia

> *Equips "Shield" magic*

## Overview

Grants no spell at level 1. At level 2 it learns Shield, a 180 MP buff that makes the target immune to every element except non-elemental damage for several turns. Unreflectable, and one of the strongest defensive effects in the game.
