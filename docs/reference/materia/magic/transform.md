# Transform Materia

> *Equips "Transform" magic*

## Overview

Inflicts or cures the Small and Frog transformations. Both spells hit allies with 100% probability, so the Materia doubles as the cure for its own statuses when Esuna is not available.

- **Buy:** North Corel, Cosmo Canyon, Gongaga, Mideel (after Ultimate Weapon)
- **Find:** Corel Bridge
- **Initially equipped:** Cait Sith
