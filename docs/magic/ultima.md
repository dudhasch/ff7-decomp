# Ultima Materia

> *Equips "Ultima" magic*

## Overview

Grants no spell at level 1. At level 2 it learns Ultima, the strongest attack magic in the game: heavy non-elemental damage to all enemies for 130 MP, with no All-Materia split penalty and no way to reflect it.

- **Find:** North Corel. Given free for stopping the runaway train; sold for 50,000 gil if the train crashes; permanently unavailable if the train is never boarded.
