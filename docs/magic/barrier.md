# Barrier Materia

> *Equips "Barrier" magic*

## Overview

Defensive buffs, and with Restore one of only two five-level Magic Materia. Barrier halves physical damage, MBarrier halves magical damage, Wall applies both at once, and Reflect bounces up to four incoming spells back at their caster.
