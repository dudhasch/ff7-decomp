# Revive Materia

> *Equips "Revive" magic*

## Overview

Resurrection. Life revives a KO'd ally with minimal HP; Life2 revives with full HP for 100 MP. Both can also damage undead enemies. The 45,000 AP step to Life2 is one of the steepest single level-ups among Magic Materia.
