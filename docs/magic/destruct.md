# Destruct Materia

> *Equips "Destruct" magic*

## Overview

Buff removal and instant death. DeBarrier strips Barrier/MBarrier/Reflect/Shield; DeSpell strips a wider list including Haste, Regen and Resist; Death is a 44% instant kill. All three are unreflectable.
