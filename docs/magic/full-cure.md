# Full Cure Materia

> *Equips "Full Cure" magic*

## Overview

Grants no spell at level 1. At level 2 it learns FullCure, which restores a target's HP completely for 99 MP and cannot be reflected. Mastering it costs 100,000 AP - among the most expensive Materia in the game.
