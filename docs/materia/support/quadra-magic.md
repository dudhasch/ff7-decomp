# Quadra Magic Materia

## Overview

Doubles total damage while leaving the MP cost untouched - the best damage-per-MP support in the game. Knights of Round is the sole Materia it refuses to link with. Quirk: if the linked spell is single-target but the character also has Mega All (or a second All link), the game shows all targets highlighted but actually casts four times at one randomly chosen target.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | [Support Materia](README.md) (blue) |
| **Max level** | 5 |
| **AP to master** | 200,000 |
| **Stat changes** | None - Support Materia never change stats |

## Links to

All Magic and Summon Materia **except Knights of Round**

## Levels

| Level | Effect | AP to reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Casts the linked Magic or Summon four times in succession at half damage each - 2x total damage for the normal MP cost. Usable once per battle. | 0 |
| 2 | Usable twice per battle. | 40,000 |
| 3 | Usable three times per battle. | 80,000 |
| 4 | Usable four times per battle. | 120,000 |
| 5 | Usable five times per battle. | 200,000 |

## How to obtain

- Materia Cave near the Mideel Area

## Note

Because of the *Master Command and Support Materia* glitch, a Support Materia linked to [Master Command](../command/master-command.md) applies its effect to every command the character can use - including plain Attack and Item.
