# Final Attack Materia

## Overview

Fires the linked Materia automatically on the wearer's death. Linked to Revive it self-resurrects; linked to Phoenix it revives the whole party while damaging the enemy - the single most famous Materia combination in the game. The spell or summon still costs MP and fails if the character has none. Linked to a Master Materia it picks at random.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | [Support Materia](README.md) (blue) |
| **Max level** | 5 |
| **AP to master** | 160,000 |
| **Stat changes** | None - Support Materia never change stats |

## Links to

All Magic and Summon Materia; Steal, Sense, Throw, Morph, Deathblow, Manipulate, Mime, Master Command

## Levels

| Level | Effect | AP to reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Uses the linked Materia when the wearer is killed. | 0 |
| 2 | Works twice per battle. | 20,000 |
| 3 | Works three times per battle. | 40,000 |
| 4 | Works four times per battle. | 80,000 |
| 5 | Works five times per battle. | 160,000 |

## How to obtain

- Battle Square special battle prize. **Missable** if the Materia stock is full the first time you win.

## Note

Because of the *Master Command and Support Materia* glitch, a Support Materia linked to [Master Command](../command/master-command.md) applies its effect to every command the character can use - including plain Attack and Item.
