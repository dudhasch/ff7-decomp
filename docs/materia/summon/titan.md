# Titan Materia

> *Summons Titan*

## Overview

Earth-elemental group damage. Like the Earth Materia it misses floating and airborne targets.

| **Castings per battle** | 1 per star (5 at master) |

## How to obtain

- Gongaga reactor. The Materia is not visible on the field, but can still be picked up any time before the North Crater events on disc 2.
