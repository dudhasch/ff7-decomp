# Phoenix Materia

> *Summons Phoenix*

## Overview

Fire damage to all enemies *and* revival of every dead ally. Famous as a Final Attack pairing: linked to Final Attack it resurrects the party when the wearer is killed. The only regular Summon Materia obtainable twice, via the Fort Condor / Bone Village oversight.

## How to obtain

- Fort Condor, at the top of the mountain - available after the Huge Materia mission if you win, or lose the minigame but defeat the boss.
- Excavate at Bone Village after the Raid on Midgar, if Buntline was previously excavated and the Fort Condor Materia was not taken.
