# Odin Materia

> *Summons Odin*

## Overview

Instant death rather than damage: Steel Bladed Sword kills every non-immune enemy with 92% success. Against an enemy immune to instant death it switches to Gunge Lance, a single-target non-elemental hit. One of only three Summon Materia with a status trait, making it the Added Effect source for Death.
