# Bahamut ZERO Materia

> *Summons Bahamut ZERO*

## Overview

The third and strongest Bahamut. Tera Flare is heavy non-elemental damage to all enemies that ignores Magic Defense.

## How to obtain

- Cosmo Canyon - the blue Huge Materia, granted if you hold both Bahamut and Neo Bahamut.
- If the Huge Materia was missed, excavate it from the Bone Village minigame.
