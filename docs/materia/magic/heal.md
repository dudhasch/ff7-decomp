# Heal Materia

> *Equips "Heal" magic*

## Overview

Status recovery. Poisona clears Poison only; Esuna clears nearly every common negative status; Resist pre-empts transformation statuses. The jump to Resist is expensive (52,000 AP), making Heal one of the slower Materia to master.
