# Gravity Materia

> *Equips "Gravity" magic*

## Overview

Fractional damage: Demi removes 25% of the target's *current* HP, Demi2 50% and Demi3 75%, independent of the caster's stats. Cannot be reflected. Many bosses are immune, but where it lands it is the most MP-efficient damage in the game.
