# Fire Materia

> *Equips "Fire" magic*

## Overview

The basic fire-elemental attack line. Linked to an Elemental Materia in weapon slots it adds fire damage to physical attacks; in armor slots it grants fire resistance, and at Materia level MASTER it absorbs fire.
