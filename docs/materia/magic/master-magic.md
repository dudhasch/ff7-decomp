# Master Magic Materia

> *Equips all magic*

## Overview

A single Materia that grants the entire Magic skillset with no stat penalties whatsoever. Creating it consumes one mastered copy of every Magic Materia, so it is usually only assembled after extensive Materia farming.

## Stat changes

None - Master Magic applies no stat penalty or bonus.

## Levels

| Level | Spell learned | AP to reach |
| --- | --- | --- |

## How to obtain

- **Find:** Cosmo Canyon - bring at least one copy of every mastered Magic Materia and examine the green Huge Materia.
- **Find:** Kalm - trade the Earth Harp.
