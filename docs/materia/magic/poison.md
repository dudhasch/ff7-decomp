# Poison Materia

> *Equips "Poison" magic*

## Overview

Poison-elemental damage with a high chance of also inflicting the Poison status (48% for Bio/Bio2, 72% for Bio3). The status damage stacks with the spell damage, making Poison a strong choice against high-HP single targets.
