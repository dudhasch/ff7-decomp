# Restore Materia

> *Equips "Restore" magic*

## Overview

The party's main healing Materia and one of only two five-level Magic Materia (with Barrier). Cure/Cure2/Cure3 restore HP on an escalating curve, while Regen at level 3 restores HP gradually over time. Commonly linked with All to heal the whole party.
